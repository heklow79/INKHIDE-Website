Questions

Good to know.

Is this real pig skin?

Yes. The proposed product uses raw pig skin sourced through a meat processor. It is intended only for tattoo practice, never for human consumption or application to a person.

Will the sheets arrive frozen?

Frozen delivery is the intended shipping method. Shipping is additional to the listed product price. Carrier acceptance, packaging performance, geographic coverage, and final rates are still being evaluated; no delivery guarantees are being made yet.

How should I handle raw pig skin?

Keep frozen until needed, thaw in the refrigerator, wear gloves, separate it from food preparation, disinfect tools and surfaces, and discard used pieces safely. Raw animal tissue may carry pathogens.

When can I order?

Not yet. We are gauging interest and confirming applicable processing, labeling, and shipping requirements before accepting orders.
